# Platform fields and limits

Checked 6 Nov 2026 against third-party guides, not the platforms' own help pages. Limits move: confirm in the app before relying on one.

| Platform | Fields | Limits | Keyword goes | Link goes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts / long-form | title, description, tags, pinned comment, chapters (long-form) | title 100 incl. hashtags (Shorts feed shows ~40); description 5,000 (first ~157 visible); tags 500 total incl. commas | title first ~40 chars; first sentence of description | pinned comment (description links are not clickable on Shorts) |
| TikTok | hook (on screen / first line), caption, hashtags | caption 2,200 via API, up to 4,000 in app; hashtags count toward it | hook and first caption sentence | profile or link-in-bio; say "link in bio" |
| Instagram Reels | cover text, caption, hashtags | caption 2,200; ~125 chars visible before "more"; hard cap 30 hashtags, advice 3 to 5 | first ~125 chars | link in bio (caption links are not clickable) |
| Facebook Reels | caption only (no title field), hashtags | check in app | first sentence acts as the title; keyword twice, naturally | pinned comment |
| X | post | 280 (free accounts) | in the sentence | in the post or a reply |
| Threads | post | 500 | in the sentence | in the post or a reply |
| LinkedIn | post | 3,000; only the first 2 lines show before "see more" | first 2 lines | in the first comment |

## Per-field rules

- **YouTube title:** `[product]: [what people search] + one curiosity trigger`, then 2 to 3 hashtags at the end picked from the Studio suggestion list. Not hashtags in the description.
- **YouTube description:** one sentence with the keyword, then short numbered steps (verb first), then the sourced numbers with check dates, then one closer. No second pitch.
- **YouTube tags:** mix of what people type (product, "how to ...", topic) plus the channel name. Leave out claims that expire ("free") unless checked on posting day.
- **TikTok:** the hook is one short honest sentence, not a headline.
- **Instagram:** cover text equals the thumbnail; the curiosity lives in the caption.
- **Facebook:** no engagement bait ("comment YES"). One real question for comments is fine.
- **Every platform:** put "affiliate link" next to any affiliate link, turn on the platform's paid-promotion or branded-content toggle, and turn on the AI label when voice or footage is synthetic.
