# Anti-slop check

## Patterns that read as AI or brochure

| Pattern | Why it fails | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Opening filler: "In today's digital age...", "Did you know that...", "Here is a list of..." | says nothing | start with the point |
| Hype words: revolutionary, game-changer, must-try, ultimate, best solution | no proof | state the result or the number |
| Em dashes and "not just X, but Y" | the most common AI tell | full stop or comma |
| Three bullets of equal length, every time | rhythm too even | vary the lengths, or use two |
| An emoji on every line | noise | at most one, only if it helps |
| Fake urgency: "don't miss out", "limited time" | untrue unless sourced | drop it |
| Stacked closers: "so what are you waiting for? Follow now!" | two promises | one closer, no new claim |
| Ad-copy sentences: "Perfect for creators who want to grow" | a pitch, not information | delete |
| Vague claims: "many people", "very fast", "super cheap" | unprovable | the number and the date checked |

## Bahasa Indonesia (casual, "aku" / "kamu")

| Jangan | Tulis |
|---|---|
| "Berikut adalah", "Langkahnya:" | "Caranya gampang:" |
| "naskah yang sudah final" | "naskah yang sudah jadi" |
| "Di era digital ini...", "Tahukah kamu bahwa..." | langsung ke inti |
| "revolusioner", "wajib coba", "solusi terbaik" | hasil nyata atau angkanya |
| "Yuk", "Mari", "Simak sampai habis!" | kalimat biasa, atau tidak ada |
| "Cocok buat kamu yang bikin konten..." | hapus |
| tanda pisah panjang (—) | titik atau koma |

## Before and after (YouTube description, English)

Before: "Discover the revolutionary ElevenLabs — the ultimate AI voice solution for creators! Here is a list of steps: 1. Sign up 2. Create. Don't miss out, subscribe now!"

After: "The voice in this video isn't a recording. It's AI voice-over from ElevenLabs. Here's how I made it: 1. Open Text to Speech 2. Pick a voice 3. Paste the script 4. Generate sentence by sentence. Fix one line? Redo just that line. The script cost 717 credits (1 character = 1 credit). The free plan gave 10,000 credits a month when I checked on 31 Oct 2026, so check the pricing page again."

## Last check

- Read it aloud. Brochure? Rewrite.
- Every number has a source and a date.
- Keyword appears in a normal sentence, not stuffed.
- One closer, no new promise.
